Why DP Coaching

DP stands for Diverse Paths, and it means exactly that: everyone who walks in here is different. Different ages, different bodies, different reasons for starting. Some pick it up fast; some need it slow and steady — both get there. There's no one way to learn boxing. There's your way, and my job is to find it. Different routes. Same goal. Same destination. Victory.

And how we get there matters. As your coach, I'm really a friend — someone in your corner, helping you understand yourself better. Boxing does that.

Gbolahan Inakoju — Founder & Head Coach

Your Path

Every path is different.
Here's how yours starts.

Step 01

First session

We meet, we move, we talk. You hit pads for the first time, I learn your goals, your body and your starting point — and we map your path.

Step 02

The basics

Stance, guard, jab, cross, footwork. The unglamorous weeks where everything that comes later is quietly built. Most people are shocked how fast it clicks.

Step 03

Progression

Combinations, defence, head movement, ring craft. Sessions get faster, sharper and harder — because now you can handle it.

Step 04

The destination

Fitter than you've been in years. Confident in a way people around you notice. And genuinely able to box — not gym-class box. Box.

The Method

The craft first.
Then we build.

Every client follows the same arc — at their own speed. That's how it works here.

New to it

Learn the craft

If you've never boxed, we start with the craft — stance, guard, your first real punches — taught properly, at a pace that respects where you're starting from. Nobody gets thrown in the deep end.

Getting comfortable

Make it feel natural

Reps, rounds and patience until the awkward stuff stops feeling awkward. Comfort comes first — it's the foundation every good habit and all real confidence gets built on.

From there

Build. Build. Build.

Once you're comfortable, we stack it — sharper combinations, harder rounds, a bigger engine. Week on week, layer on layer. That's the point where training stops being a chore and starts being addictive.
