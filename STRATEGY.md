# Diverse Paths — Operating Strategy

*Maintained by your technical co-founder. Everything here exists to move one of:
revenue, profit, leads, brand value, retention, automation, time saved, client experience.*

## Positioning

**Premium boxing coaching for ordinary people.** Not a fight gym, not a bootcamp.
The promise: real technique, real fitness, real confidence — on whatever path the
client is on. The brand line does the work everywhere: **Different paths. One destination.**

We compete on coaching quality and experience, never on price. Small numbers on
purpose (1-to-1 and groups capped at 4) is a feature — say it constantly.

## The offer ladder

| Rung | Offer | Price (launch) | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intro session | £20, credited to first block | Kill the risk of saying yes |
| 2 | Foundations — 6-week programme | £249 (12 sessions) | Productised on-ramp for complete beginners; cohort = built-in retention |
| 3 | Small group (max 4) | £25 / person / session | Volume + social proof + friends-bring-friends |
| 4 | 1-to-1 coaching | £60 / session · 10 for £540 | Flagship margin; priority slots + video feedback justify premium |

Blocks and programmes are paid up front — cash flow first. Raise prices when a
week is >80% booked; never discount, add value instead (extra video review, a
guest session for a friend).

## The funnel (what the Scoreboard measures)

Instagram reach → profile visits → DMs (keyword **"PATH"**) → intro booked → client.

Weakest link rule: each Sunday the worst conversion percentage is the only
marketing problem we work on that week.
- Low reach → hooks/format problem (content)
- Low reach→visits → content interesting but not personal enough (more face, more client stories)
- Low visits→DMs → bio/link problem (profile, pinned posts, site)
- Low DMs→intro → reply speed or offer clarity
- Low intro→client → session experience or follow-up (should be rare)

Reply to every DM within an hour where humanly possible. Speed is the cheapest
conversion upgrade that exists.

## 90-day plan

**Days 1–30 — Consistency + conversion plumbing.**
Post the 30-day Fight Plan (The Corner). Put the site link in bio. Standardise
"DM PATH". Log the Scoreboard every Sunday. Collect the first 3 real testimonials
and swap them into the site.

**Days 31–60 — Proof + product.**
Launch the first Foundations cohort off the back of week-4 content. Film every
client win (with consent) — transformation content becomes the dominant pillar.
Second 30-day content block, weighted by what the Scoreboard says worked.

**Days 61–90 — Price + leverage.**
Raise 1-to-1 pricing if capacity is filling. Introduce referral mechanic: existing
client brings a friend to a small-group session free; friend converts → client gets
a free session. Start an email/WhatsApp list of every lead who didn't convert —
monthly "paths" newsletter with one client story + one open slot count.
